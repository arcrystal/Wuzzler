Privacy policy

Wuzzler Daily respects your privacy.

Effective August 28, 2026

Information stored on your device

Wuzzler Daily stores puzzle progress, completion times, streaks, settings, reminder preferences, and tutorial state locally on your device. This information is used to run the app and is not sent to Wuzzler Daily.

Optional Game Center features

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity, friends authorization, leaderboard scores, and achievements under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Wuzzler Daily uses that information only to show Game Center features and submit eligible daily results. You can play every puzzle without signing in.

Notifications

If you enable a daily reminder, iOS stores and delivers that notification locally. You can change or disable reminders in Wuzzler Daily settings or system settings.

Puzzle updates and website hosting

The app may request a static puzzle file from this GitHub Pages website. GitHub may process standard request information needed to deliver and secure the site. Wuzzler Daily does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

TestFlight feedback

During beta testing, you may voluntarily send screenshots, comments, or crash feedback through TestFlight. Apple processes that information and makes it available to the developer for diagnosing the issue you report.

Your choices

You can play as a guest, decline Game Center friends access, disable notifications, or remove local Wuzzler Daily data by deleting the app. Wuzzler Daily does not sell personal information.

Contact

Beta testers can contact the developer with TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback feature. See the beta support page for instructions.
