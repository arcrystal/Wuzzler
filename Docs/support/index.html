Beta support

Help make Wuzzler Daily better.

For the fastest response during the beta, send feedback from TestFlight. This automatically includes the app version and device details that help diagnose a problem.

Send written or screenshot feedback

  1. Open the TestFlight app.
  2. Select Wuzzler Daily.
  3. Tap Send Beta Feedback.

You can also take a screenshot while using Wuzzler Daily and choose Share Beta Feedback.

Report a gameplay issue

Include the game name, puzzle date, what you expected, and what happened. For loading or haptic issues, mention your device model and whether it was the first game opened after launching the app.

Before reporting missing rankings

Game Center is optional. Friends, global rankings, and achievements require a Game Center sign-in, but puzzles and local progress do not.
